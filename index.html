This is my story.

2018–19

Where it started

Before everything, I started content creation from a Jio keypad 4G phone. Literally I made my 1st YouTube channel with this phone — it was education category, and I just used to shoot video of my notes by standing the JioPhone at a box. Then I uploaded, and I wrote each title, description and even the tags with the keypad. I uploaded around 20 long videos. This was around 2018 or 19. I used this keypad phone until I could buy a second-hand Android phone for ₹3000.

2020

Fourteen, and my first job

So I was 14, when my financial condition became worst. My father and mother both were ill when the lockdown started in India around 2020. That was the first time I started working for someone. I went to the local market to ask for any job, and I got one at a general store for ₹4000 / $42 per month. The job was really tough for me. I had no idea about the world at that time.

Also at that time YT Shorts was newly launched. I used to play games, so I made a gaming YouTube channel and uploaded shorts daily. By my hard work and consistency I reached 230k+ subscribers. But I was so immature and had no idea how to convert it into money — so I just got stuck with a little Shorts fund.

For around 2 months I handled my family. My mom and dad became healthy again after 1.5 to 2 months, and my father started handling the family. After that my YouTube channel started getting dead — because I was totally immature, I couldn’t understand who my actual audience was, or how they could bring me profits.

2020–21

Learning to earn

Then I got a friend who was running FB ads, and I got connected with him. I knew editing by then, because I had been creating content for the last 2 years. So I edited his videos and he closed the leads. We earned a little money from there too. Then that also stopped.

2022

The Mumbai decision

The problem was, my father’s earning was not enough. So in 2022 I decided I would go to Mumbai — to see the world, to know the real world, and to support my family financially. My family was really afraid, because I had never gone out of my district in my life and had never visited anywhere on my own. But I took the risk and went to Mumbai.

Here I will not tell what I struggled in Mumbai. I never told anyone — not even my family.

My education also got left behind. I just became a 10th dropout. It’s not an issue now — this year I’m continuing open schooling and going for a bachelor’s degree.

2026
